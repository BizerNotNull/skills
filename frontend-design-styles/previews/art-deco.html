The house

A room built for one screen

"The ceiling is the architecture; everything else is just seating."

Six hundred and forty seats in two tiers, a plaster ceiling from 1928 and an organ chamber nobody has been able to open since 1991.

Before the feature

The bar opens at five and closes at the interval, and the short films run without advertising because we have never sold any.

Members may reserve the two brass boxes up to ten days ahead. The rest of the house is unreserved and always has been.
